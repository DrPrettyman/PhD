# Message from Valerie:

- One needs to know about early warning signals and spectral properties
  of various noises in order to study tipping.

- I suggest that you focus on the main idea of multidimensional weather
  systems, where you could try to model and analyse data (hurricanes is
  one of applications, with data in hands; later we could investigate
  others).

- It would be good if you made a list of bullet points, each for a month
  research or so, towards this goal. Something like:

  1.  analyse components of time series and write down a stochastic
      model for each

  2.  check literature for existing models of pressure/wind speed and
      formulate a 2D model with your components taken into account

  3.  obtain precise form of the model from the observed data (it may be
      Kalman filter, or some other technique of parameter estimation, of
      your choice

  4.  attempt to derive eigenvalues and see if there is early warning
      signal in this system.

- If you plan this well week by week and stick to the plan, you can get
  a result by Easter, I think. A habit of systematic work is essential
  during PhD course.
