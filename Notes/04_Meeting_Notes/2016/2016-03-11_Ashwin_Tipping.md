# Ashwin

Ashwin (ashwin2012) "usefully" categorises tipping effects in open
systems:

- B-tipping, in which the output changes abruptly (well surely tipping
  is all about abruptly changing output?) owing to a bifurcation of a
  quasi-static attractor. or **Bifurcation induced**

- N-tipping, in which noisy fluctuations result in the system departing
  from a neighbourhood of a quasi-static attractor. or **Noise induced**

- R-tipping, in which the system fails to track a continuously changing
  quasi-static attractor. By "continuously changing", we mean that the
  attractor moves, in a $C^0$ way.
