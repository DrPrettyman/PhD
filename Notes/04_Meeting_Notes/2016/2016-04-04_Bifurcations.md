# Bifurcations

## Saddle-node bifurcation

For example $$
\dot{x} &= \mu - x^2\\
\dot{y} &= -y
$$ has a stable fixed point at $(\sqrt{\mu},0)$ and an
unstable (saddle) node at $(-\sqrt{\mu}, 0)$. As $\mu$ decreases to zero
the two fixed points meet. When $\mu<0$ there is no longer a fixed
point.

## Pitchfork bifurcation

- **Transcritical** A saddle node and a stable node collide. At the
  point they collide there is no fixed point, then they reappear but
  have "switched" their properties.

- **Supercritical** A stable node "splits" into two stable node and
  leaves behind a saddle node.

- **Subcritical** Two saddle nodes and a stable node collide and become
  a saddle node.

## Hopf bifurcation

- **Supercritical** The stable point at the center of a spiral grows
  into a stable limit cycle with an unstable fixed point at the center.

- **Subcritical** An unstable limit cycle around a stable fixed point
  shrinks until in becomes a single point (unstable) and eliminates the
  stable fixed point.

## Homoclinic bifurcation

A limit cycle collides with a saddle node and breaks the loop.
