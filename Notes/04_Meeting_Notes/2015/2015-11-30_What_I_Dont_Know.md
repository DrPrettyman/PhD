# Decay rate

I think that **decay rate** (of series $X$ for example) refers to the
rate at which $$
ACF_{\text{lag}~n}(X)\xrightarrow[n\rightarrow\infty]{}0.
$$ The rate at which the lag-$n$ autocorrelations decay to
zero as $n\rightarrow\infty$.
