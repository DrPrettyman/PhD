\begin{letter}{Co-editor \\ Europhysics Letters}
\opening{Dear Sir or Madam:}

I am writing to you with regards to the enclosed submission to EPL. We, the authors, believe that the method introduced in this paper is original: while it is already known that the power spectrum (PS) scaling exponent is related to other indicators of early warning signals (EWS's) in dynamical systems, the ``PS-indicator'' has not itself been used before to provide an EWS. The application of this method to tropical cyclones is also original: EWS indicators have previously been applied to dynamical systems with multiple stable states. We are therefore able to report an original result concerning tropical cyclones, and to introduce a method which has the possibility of being applied in contexts where previously published methods may fail.

We believe that the method introduced here will be applicable in many areas of physics. Certainly there is the prospect of deepening understanding in areas where previously published EWS indicators have been used, and there is also the possibility of broadening the research to different dynamical systems where previous methods have not proved useful. In particular, we hope to stimulate further research in areas of geophysics influenced by climate change, including extreme weather events, where predictability is an essential problem.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

I look forward to your reply.

\closing{Yours Faithfully,}

\encl{Submission entitled {\it A novel scaling indicator of early warning signals helps anticipate tropical cyclones}.}
